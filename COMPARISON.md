# Scope and comparison

Candidate targets: https://senja.io/ and https://appsumo.com/products/feedspace/ . These sources were identified during factory research. Recheck current vendor capabilities and pricing before publishing a comparative claim.

Our locally tested workflow: written testimonial collection, explicit publication consent, owner moderation, public iframe wall, CSV export, and contributor-controlled withdrawal. Hosting and operating costs remain with the operator. No subscription entitlement checks exist in this implementation.

This release does not replace video capture, managed hosting, team collaboration, review-site importing, email campaigns, analytics, or enterprise features. The comparison is a scope statement, not an audited vendor feature matrix.

Proposed launch line: “Collect, approve and embed written testimonials on your own server—with a private removal link for every contributor.”
